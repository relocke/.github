# ReLocke community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in ReLocke open, constructive, and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical familiarity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and generous in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas and implementations rather than people.
- Welcome questions and make space for new contributors.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and respect privacy.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method published at [relocke.io](https://relocke.io). Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is officially representing ReLocke.
